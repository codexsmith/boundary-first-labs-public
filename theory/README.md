# Boundary Theory — Bounded Research Program

Boundary Theory investigates whether recurring patterns of distinction, representation, boundary, admissibility, transformation, closure, defect, and repair can be formalized and compared without replacing native domain theories.

Candidate formalisms and research objects include Distinction Space, Representational Mechanics, formal/typed grammars, and constructive representations. Their scope, usefulness, and exact relationships remain subject to proof, counterexample, literature comparison, and independent scrutiny.

**Important separations**

- Institution is not method; method is not theory; theory is not apparatus or instrument.
- Analogy is not equivalence. Cross-domain resemblance is a research question, not proof.
- Executable checks are evidence only for their declared assumptions and scope.
- A mathematical correspondence is not automatically a physical prediction.

For a concise set of falsifiable and comparative research questions, see [Open Research Questions](OPEN_RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md).

This folder introduces the public research program; detailed claims belong to a disclosed, versioned [project release](../projects/README.md), with [research area](../research/README.md) navigation and [publication controls](../PUBLICATION_POLICY.md).
