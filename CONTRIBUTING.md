# Contributing and Criticism

Boundary First Labs welcomes specific corrections, counterexamples, reproducibility failures, prior-art references, clarity suggestions, and accessibility feedback.

For now, **GitHub issues are the preferred public feedback channel**. Code or content contributions require a maintainer discussion and an explicit, compatible rights review before acceptance. Public issues and pull requests are visible to others.

A useful issue includes:

- The relevant file, commit, or statement.
- Its declared source or status, and the precise disputed assumption.
- A counterexample, source reference, or reproducible witness when possible.
- Whether the concern is factual, mathematical, methodological, licensing, implementation, security, or accessibility-related.

Please do **not** submit secrets, unpublished third-party materials, confidential data, sensitive personal records, or identifying information about others. Do not publicly share private Lab or ChatGPT conversation links.

Submitting an issue or pull request does not automatically transfer copyright or grant BFL license rights in the submission. Maintainers must agree on contributor permission and the target artifact's release license before merging substantial contributions.

The Lab considers negative results and demonstrated limits useful. A correction does not need to support a broader theory to be valuable.

Read [Intellectual Provenance](INTELLECTUAL_PROVENANCE.md), [Publication Policy](PUBLICATION_POLICY.md), and [Licensing and Rights](LICENSING.md) before proposing new material.
