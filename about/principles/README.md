# Institutional Principles

**Public institutional identity, ethics, and strategic commitments.**

This is the public home for individually approved Boundary First Labs principles. It is not a publication of internal notes, private word banks, raw conversations, or working governance records.

## Published principle

- **[Collaboration by Construction](collaboration-by-construction.md)** — the engineering commitment that gives practical meaning to **“Collaboration over domination.”** It describes the boundary conditions we seek to make explicit in research, software, human-facing tools, and institutional systems.

## How to read these statements

Principles establish **values, intentions, and design obligations**. They do not certify that every BFL project has implemented those obligations or that every proposed design has been proven to work. Each technical or scientific claim still requires its own evidence, limitations, and correction path.

Public principles should be understandable outside the Lab, open to challenge, and specific enough to inform decisions. A motto is useful only to the extent that people can inspect whether the practice lives up to it.
