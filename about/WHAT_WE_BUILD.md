# What Boundary First Labs Builds

**Public institutional orientation — descriptive, not a product launch.**

Boundary First Labs combines research, software and systems engineering, and public-facing explanation around one practical demand: systems should be inspectable at the boundaries where their representations become consequential.

## Three connected activities

**Research.** Investigate formal and empirical questions, reconstruct established results when they provide a strong comparison, keep hypotheses separate from findings, and preserve negative tests and unresolved obligations.

**Instruments.** Develop ways to make complex representations easier to inspect: executable demonstrations, source-linked evidence, typed transformation maps, reproducibility checks, and repair-oriented technical tools.

**Application.** Explore how those methods can support software design, accountable automation, public-system analysis, scientific inquiry, and human-facing systems without bypassing native expertise or decision authority.

## Distinctions we insist on

- **Boundary First** is a portable method; **Boundary Theory** is its bounded formal investigation, not the institution's only activity.
- A mathematical result may be inherited; a BFL-specific contribution may be a better representation, integration, instrument or implementation, rather than a new theorem.
- A prototype or analysis can inform someone's judgment without being authorized to take action in the world.
- An open repository makes material available to inspect, but does not license all protected expression for reuse.
