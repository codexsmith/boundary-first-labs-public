# ADR-0005 — Proportionate Public Publication Gates

**Date:** 2026-11-23  
**Status:** adopted in private Lab governance; public policy projection  
**Scope:** reader-facing review routes and disclosure boundary only

## Context

Following withdrawal of a detailed mathematical educational example, a blanket founder line-by-line gate was introduced into public copy. That protected sensitive technical material but accidentally constrained unrelated public doctrine, AI governance work, institutional analysis and civic explanation.

## Decision

Use **P1** normal editorial review for cleared public doctrine and commentary, **P2** targeted specialist review for consequential domain-specific claims, and **P3** exact founder disclosure approval for confidential technical work. Classification follows actual content rather than the subject title, source folder or incidental technical vocabulary.

A document may contain both public and confidential material. Publish a truthful separated public explanation if possible; hold sensitive sections or the entire piece where separation would mislead.

## Authority and existing decisions

The private Lab's publication-governance controls own operational routing, claim status and the actual authority to release. This ADR records a **public explanation**, not an independent second publication-control system.

ADR-0001's single-owner public project routes, ADR-0002's legal rights separation, and ADR-0003's internal-history firewall continue to apply.

No previously withdrawn technical artifact is republished by this decision. No private screening criteria, software, algorithm, formula, research packet, code or proprietary implementation detail are released here.

## Verification

An AI-responsibility essay without protected mechanisms may follow P1. A quantitative high-stakes analysis may need P2. A confidential technical derivation cannot enter public Git without specific P3 approval, even within general prose. The public [Publication Policy](../../PUBLICATION_POLICY.md) and [Preflight Checklist](../templates/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md) should reflect those distinctions.
