## 2. Notable source and claim distinctions

### Human Viability Engineering: selected Nov 15 source

The working finite-flow demonstrator makes *individual resource access*, institutional gates and declared floors explicit. Six synthetic cases contrast feasible access, gate-obstructed access, permission repair, alternative-route repair, physical scarcity, and a financial record that cannot create physical water.

**What survives:** bounded network-feasibility reasoning and previously reported local tests.  
**What does not follow:** universal viability theorem, temporal/adversarial control guarantee, objectively calibrated human-survival floor, legal permission, or deployment-ready policy.  
**Cross-program bridge:** prospective to both Economic Water Model and the newer viability-control/wicked-systems framing; no mathematical equivalence or scientific relation edge has been owner-admitted.
