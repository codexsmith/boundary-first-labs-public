## Research-event family highlights for selection

### A. RH Pass025: refuse malformed or incomplete proof witnesses

The preceding Pass024 adversarial audit (owner **source date Nov7**) identified a specific defect: the legacy Schur budget aggregator accepted altered bounds and intervals while retaining trusted success flags. The new Pass025 working prototype (owner **Nov8**) checks fully serialized rational interval entries, shapes, ordering, symmetry, pinned hashes, norm bounds and nominal matrix algebra rather than treating a `PASS` flag as mathematical evidence.

The historic target requires **11,595 interval records** in the owner's declared matrix interface; earlier sources did not preserve all M and C entrywise outward enclosures. The prototype consequently does **not** accept the historic certificate. Its local synthetic positive/negative checks validate fail-closed syntax/algebra behavior only, not transcendent rounding or the Riemann hypothesis.

**Review value:** one of the clearest examples of a model/tool discovering its own trust-boundary failure and specifying a reproducible repair.

### B. Lawful cross-domain adaptation + CDAR-001

The founder-adopted **working doctrine** (Nov8) defines a crossing from one regime to another as a typed, use-relative obligation involving preserved structures, loss, uncertainty, evidence, and authority. It explicitly does not identify every boundary with an interface or every interface with an admissible passage.

The **distinct implementation** CDAR-001 (Nov8 owner packet, integrated Nov9) models physical temperature trace → analog sensor → quantizer → inferred interval → model-relative consequence → fictional authority gate → **suggested action only**. Six synthetic fixtures include hidden excursions and calibration/source inadequacy. Two traces may yield equal samples while differing in physical consequence. The oracle uses a synthetic known trace unavailable to a real sensor.

**Review value:** demonstrate the difference between “the model returned a number” and “the system has evidence and authority for the conclusion.” This is not a certified field device or a public Codexware action engine.

### C. Life / ASM: state quotient versus continuation

The Nov8 Life Pass01 reconstructs finite dynamics and includes an offline browser visual companion, with owner-local tests and negative controls. Its Nov8 Pass02 follow-on, committed Nov9, compares finite indexed parallel-update semantics and records a glider collision whose **single observation** follows an XOR table.

The author does **not** claim a composable XOR logic gate: the nonempty output locations differ and no stable output port / fanout / downstream gate has been verified.

**Review value:** a small approachable demonstration of **why identical present observations can be insufficient for future composition**.

### D. Alpha / information: a scientifically useful negative result

The Nov8 GlueX information/covariance experiment uses published systematics plus declared illustrative correlation and approximate energy-shape assumptions. Its owner reports 24 local tests and independent-implementation sensitivity checks, but **did not have the cross-section data needed for a source-faithful empirical fit**. The Nov8 research-arc closure explicitly concludes that no universal physical information-loss rate equal to α, or first-principles value of α, emerged.

**Review value:** source-calibration honesty and a principled stop gate. Numerical resemblance to α is not physical identification.

### E. ABT bit-realization and guarded bootstrap

A Nov8 finite calibration uses explicitly typed interpretation and mode guards to distinguish a Boolean semantics from physical bit encoding, readable versus unavailable states, hold/write authority, and synthetic off-to-ready transition. Owner record reports ten local tests and eight acceptance groups; no hardware power-on or historical Bricken equivalence is established.

**Review value:** software and semantic distinctions do not by themselves specify physical execution or legal authority.

### F. CABP Pass05: guarded finite repair

Integer-equivalent forest states can disagree on a Boolean conclusion; preserving or adding a distinction may be necessary for a permitted continuation. **Review value:** a compact demonstration of dependency inversion and a repair that must be **adequate for the intended consequence**, not just formally well-typed.
