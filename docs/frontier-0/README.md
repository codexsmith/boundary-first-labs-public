## What Frontier 0 does and does not establish

**Confirmed in this public repository:** the n-ball educational example is a published, versioned public project with a `release.yaml` manifest. This is **not a novel theorem, independent scientific validation, or deployed interactive service**.

**Current presentation seeds:** seven other program/method/product facets are identified by the founder's visible instructions and public navigation. Their implementation/evidence/disclosure status remains to be reconciled against the canonical Lab owners. The actual in-flight public Codexware MVP is explicitly a first-priority verification gap, **not** a product we may reconstruct by guessing.

**No private-source mining has been performed by this public artifact.** Earlier chats, unpublished diagrams, source-code repositories, experiment results and internal registry state are not exported here. The absence of a public artifact is not evidence that the underlying research does not exist.

## How to read the manifest

The `public_nodes` and `navigation_edges` fields are presentation **routing**, not mathematical or scientific dependencies. They do not establish cross-domain equivalence.

`research_relation_candidates` begins empty. A claimed refinement, equivalence, predecessor or support edge requires original source references, source-owner review, and appropriate evidence before being represented here.

`facet_coverage.coverage_state=seed_only` means an initial public-description seed, **not** a completed chronological wave. `selected_public_release_verified` is used only for the public n-ball package, and does not imply comprehensive recovery of the mathematics, physics or computation corpus.
