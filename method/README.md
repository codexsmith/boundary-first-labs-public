# Boundary First — Method

**Boundary First** is the Lab's portable analytical and engineering method. It is **not identical to Boundary Theory**, and practical success does not by itself verify a generalized mathematical theory.

A public working sequence:

1. Identify the system, its purpose, and the relevant state.
2. Make representations, distinctions, boundaries, and assumptions explicit.
3. Specify permitted transformations, contracts, invariants, and witnesses.
4. Check what a representation forgets and which consequences remain observable.
5. Detect defects, record uncertainty, and identify justified repair paths.
6. Compare outcomes with established domain-native methods.

This is a practical public explanation, not a claim that BFL invented state machines, control theory, invariants, or formal methods.

For theory development and open questions, see [Theory](../theory/README.md). For actual release evidence, follow a [project](../projects/README.md).
