# Claim, Contribution, and Nonclaim Record

**Project genre:** public educational calibration. The original source-side research remains unpromoted; this project makes **no novel mathematical theorem claim**.

| NB-07 | This representation improves mathematical problem solving across domains | **Unestablished research question** | No matched-baseline study, blind evaluation, or independent external verification here |

## What BFL is specifically adding *in this release*

A readable *crosswalk* of known operations and their admissibility/representation boundaries, organized around a single Euclidean-ball family and one explicit counterexample to scalar sufficiency. The selection, ordering, and explanation of those distinctions is this particular BFL public presentation; the mathematical content remains inherited.

## Explicit nonclaims

- No ownership of Gamma identities, Euclidean balls, Steiner's formula, classical convex geometry, or any mathematical equation *as such*.
- No new theorem about n-balls, their topology, fractional dimension, quantum mechanics, gravity, or the foundations of mathematics.
- No implication that \(\partial^2=0\) proves that arbitrary diagrams commute, that a product of rods is a Euclidean disk, or that volume determines shape.
- No inference that an analytic Gamma parameter represents a physical geometry in noninteger dimension.
- No assertion that successful numerical checks constitute a proof assistant certificate, independent peer review, or validation of Boundary Theory.
- No evidence yet that this typed presentation outperforms conventional mathematical exposition, symbolic-algebra systems, or formal knowledge graphs.

## Competing baselines and discriminating test

The native baselines are elementary analysis/geometry textbooks, typed programming representations, standard convex geometry, and existing mathematical knowledge-graph or proof-assistant tools. A useful next trial would compare a specified task requiring *recovery of a lost continuation* across equally informed readers or checkers, with the same underlying mathematics and a controlled representation. That experiment has **not** been executed.
