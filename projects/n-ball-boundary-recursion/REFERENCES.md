# Mathematical Sources and Prior Art

This note's mathematical identities are classical. Links below are **references**, not assertions that their texts, images, typesetting, or code have been copied or relicensed.

1. **NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions — Gamma functional relations, §5.5.** The recurrence \(\Gamma(z+1)=z\Gamma(z)\) and related identities.  
   https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.5

2. **Math, 2022 — “Novel Recurrence Relations for Volumes and Surfaces of n-Balls…”** Section 2 explicitly identifies the ordinary n-ball volume formula and dimensional two-step recurrence as *known formulas*.  
   https://doi.org/10.3390/math10132212

3. **Steiner's formula for planar convex offsets**, Theorem 5 as stated in “Improved Description of Blaschke–Santaló Diagrams via Numerical Shape Optimization” (*Applied Mathematics & Optimization*, 2025).  
   https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00245-025-10250-w

4. **D. Richeson — “Volumes of n-dimensional balls” (2010).** Accessible mathematical exposition including the standard scalar recurrence and parity seeds.  
   https://divisbyzero.com/2010/05/09/volumes-of-n-dimensional-balls/
