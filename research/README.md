# Research Areas

These are **reader-facing discovery routes**, not four competing canonical research stores. A release has one project owner and can be indexed under several areas. Each area retains its native standards for validation.

- [Mathematics and formal systems](mathematics/README.md) — proofs, counterexamples, constructions, logic, and formal comparisons.
- [Physics and natural systems](physics/README.md) — models, measurements, empirical constraints, and physical interpretations.
- [Computing and engineered systems](computing/README.md) — programs, architectures, invariants, executable tests, and benchmarks.
- [Consequential and institutional systems](consequential-systems/README.md) — accounting, law, governance, organizations, and other systems with domain-specific normative or empirical constraints.

A cross-domain question may appear in several indexes, but its released files should not be duplicated. Link to its [canonical public project package](../projects/README.md).

Nothing in these empty guides represents an approved theorem, field result, or published paper.
